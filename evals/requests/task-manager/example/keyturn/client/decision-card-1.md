# Decision: How long should people stay signed in?

- Status: answered
- Related: R-2, decision 0003

## Summary

We need to know how long a sign-in lasts before people must sign in again. We recommend 7 days. If you do not answer, we keep working on everything else, and signing in waits for your answer.

## Context

After signing in, people stay signed in until they sign out or until the sign-in runs out. A longer time is more convenient; a shorter time limits the damage if someone uses a shared or lost computer that is still signed in.

## Options

1. **7 days (recommended)**: people sign in about once a week. If a signed-in device is lost, it stays signed in for up to 7 days.
2. **1 day**: people sign in every day. A lost device stays signed in for at most one day.
3. **Until the browser is closed**: people sign in every time they open the browser. Safest on shared computers, least convenient.

## Our recommendation

7 days: you and a few friends use your own devices, so convenience matters more than the risk of shared computers (R-2).

## If you do not answer

We keep building the parts that do not depend on it. Signing in (R-2) is not finished until you answer: we never choose for you, not even our recommendation.

## Your answer

- Answer: 7 days. "We all use our own phones and laptops."
